## Dataset Creation Statement

Enygma declares that all sample files in this package were created and prepared by Enygma:

- `data/footprints.geojson`: 131 tree canopy cluster outlines delineated by Enygma. WGS 84 (RFC 7946).
- `data/dsm.tif`: a research sample clipped from Digital Surface Model data produced from Enygma's own drone survey.
- `data/dtm.tif`: a research sample clipped from Digital Terrain Model data produced from Enygma's own drone survey.
- `models/tree_trunk.glb`: normalized unit trunk mesh.
- `models/tree_canopy.glb`: normalized unit canopy dome mesh.
