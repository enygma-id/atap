## 3D Asset Description

- `tree_trunk.glb`: Normalized $1 \times 1 \times 1\,\text{m}$ unit cylinder bark trunk mesh, pivot at base $Z=0$.
- `tree_canopy.glb`: Normalized $1 \times 1 \times 1\,\text{m}$ unit foliage dome canopy mesh, pivot at base $Z=0$.

These 3D assets serve as standard normalized instancing templates for ATAP individual tree visualization (`ScenegraphLayer` and `Tile3DLayer`).
