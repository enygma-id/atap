## Dataset creation statement

Enygma declares that all three sample files were created and prepared by Enygma:

- `data/footprints.geojson`: 37 building footprints manually delineated by Enygma. The file includes collection-level provenance metadata and per-feature attribution properties.
- `data/dsm.tif`: a research sample clipped from Digital Surface Model data produced from Enygma's own drone survey.
- `data/dtm.tif`: a research sample clipped from Digital Terrain Model data produced from Enygma's own drone survey.

This release updates GeoJSON attribution metadata without changing its original polygon geometry or coordinates. DSM and DTM raster files remain unchanged.

These statements document the publisher's declared provenance. The presence of this notice or metadata does not by itself establish legal ownership or replace original survey and production records.
