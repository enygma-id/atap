# Novelty Hypothesis

ATAP's potential novelty should not be framed as novelty of generic raster
polygonization, clustering, DSM/DTM processing, or building extrusion.

Those areas have substantial prior art.

The stronger working hypothesis is the combination of:

1. GIS-native canonical representation;
2. hierarchical/nested building-mass decomposition;
3. terrain-referenced vertical semantics;
4. ordinary polygon geometry as the persistent representation;
5. renderer-independent downstream 3D interpretation.

Working statement:

> ATAP explores a GIS-native approach in which hierarchical building-mass
> complexity is retained as polygon geometry, parent/child structure, and
> vertical semantics, rather than requiring an explicit 3D model as the
> canonical representation.

This remains a hypothesis until prior-art review and validation are sufficiently
complete.

Avoid unsupported claims such as "first ever" or "world first."
