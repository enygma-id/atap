# Founding Team

**ATAP: ATAP Terrain-Aware Architectural Polygonization**

ATAP was founded within **Enygma**.

Legal entity:

**PT Enygma Solusi Negeri**

This file records the project's founding initiators and their founding roles.

Founding recognition is historical and remains separate from future employment
status, repository access, maintainer authority, or publication authorship.

## Erick Karya: Founding Vision & Research Lead

**Role at founding:** CEO, Enygma

Founding contributions:

- originated the ATAP concept and initiated the project;
- defined the initial problem and fit-for-purpose direction;
- established GIS-native canonical representation as the core research
  principle;
- led conceptual refinement toward hierarchical nested decomposition and
  terrain-referenced vertical semantics;
- established the Community / Enterprise direction and open-source research
  strategy;
- directed the transition from working prototype toward reproducible public
  software.

## Galang Mahendra: Founding Implementation Lead

**Role at founding:** Supervisor Tech, Enygma

Founding contributions:

- translated the original concept into the first working implementation;
- demonstrated the initial elevation-processing and polygonization workflow on
  real project data;
- continued implementation refinement after the research direction matured;
- led the initial software-packaging effort so ATAP could be cloned, installed,
  executed, tested, and extended more conveniently.

## Founding Recognition

Erick Karya and Galang Mahendra remain historically recognized as ATAP founding
initiators even if they later change roles, leave Enygma, become inactive, or
cease to be maintainers.

Operational authority is governed by [`GOVERNANCE.md`](./GOVERNANCE.md).
